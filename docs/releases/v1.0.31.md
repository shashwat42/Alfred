# Alfred 1.0.31

- Choose a signed-in device by name under **Settings → Schedule Notifications → Reminder device**. Open Alfred on each device with Cloud Sync enabled to add it to the list.
- Closing Alfred's main window on Linux keeps reminders and Quick Overlay available in the background. Reopen the window from the app launcher or choose **Quit Alfred** in Settings to exit.
- Click outside the conversation or its controls to dismiss Quick Overlay. On Linux, Quick Overlay opens at the top of the work area.

Device selection requires the matching backend update. Windows x64 and Fedora x86_64 use their signed in-app update channels. The Android assistant supports tap-outside dismissal and device selection in the separately signed Android build.
